The Journey of Learning Web Development

Posted on April 26, 2025

Web development is an exciting and ever-evolving field. Starting with basic HTML and CSS, we began to build small projects like personal websites and landing pages. As we learned Bootstrap, designing beautiful, responsive layouts became much easier and faster.

With each project, we faced new challenges, but every difficulty taught us valuable lessons. Whether it's understanding layouts, creating forms, or making interactive designs, every step has been a part of our growth. This blog is dedicated to sharing the journey, the struggles, the victories, and everything we continue to learn along the way.
